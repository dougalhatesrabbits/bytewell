Quick answers

Where do you cover?

Liverpool and surrounding areas, including the Wirral, Sefton, and Knowsley. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

How quickly can you get to me?

We reply the same day and can often visit within 24 to 48 hours. Many issues can be fixed remotely, which is usually even quicker.

Do you offer remote support?

Yes. For software problems, email issues, and many computer faults, we can fix things over a secure remote connection.

How much do you charge?

You get a clear price before any work begins. Home customers can choose a fixed price or hourly rate. Businesses can choose pay as you go or a monthly plan.

What if you can't fix it?

Then you don't pay for the repair. We'll tell you honestly if something isn't worth fixing and talk you through your options.

Will my data be safe?

Yes. We back up important data before significant changes and never access anything we don't need to.

Do you support Windows and Mac?

Yes, plus iPhones, iPads, Android devices, printers, routers, and smart home gear.

Is it one-off help or ongoing support only?

Both. There's no obligation to sign up for anything.
